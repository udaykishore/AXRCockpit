Introduction

• AXRCockpit is an app that allows you to wirelessly connect an HMD and a tablet to perform functions such as screen mirroring, app execution/termination, and debugging.
• Pairing: The process of registering a tablet with the HMD.
• Connect: The process of connecting the HMD and the tablet.
• Mirroring: When the HMD and tablet are connected, the HMD's screen is displayed in real-time at that location.
• Console: After connecting the HMD and tablet, you can remotely operate the HMD using shell commands.

Connection Steps

1. Pairing

• This is a one-time process to connect the HMD and the tablet.
• The HMD and tablet must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
1. After wearing the HMD, select [Settings - Developer options - Wireless debugging].
2. Press "Disabled" to change it to "Enabled".

3. Select "Pair device with pairing code".
4. After launching the AXRCockpit app on the tablet, select the PAIR button.
5. Enter the IP, Port, and Pair key displayed on the HMD into the tablet and select Yes.
6. Pairing is complete when the "Paring successful!" toast pop-up appears on the tablet.

2. Connect

• This is the process to connect the HMD and tablet after pairing is complete.
1. After wearing the HMD, go to the [Settings - Developer options - Wireless debugging] menu.
2. Check the IP and Port displayed on the screen.

3. After launching the AXRCockpit app on the tablet, select the CONNECT button.
5. Enter the IP and Port displayed on the HMD into the tablet and select Yes.
6. The connection is complete when the "Connected!" toast pop-up appears on the tablet.

Reconnection

• After pairing the HMD and tablet, you can reconnect by pressing the Connect button.
• The HMD and tablet must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
1. After wearing the HMD, select [Settings - Developer options - Wireless debugging].
2. Press "Disabled" to change it to "Enabled".

3. After wearing the HMD, go to the [Settings - Developer options - Wireless debugging] menu.
4. Check the IP and Port displayed on the screen.
5. After launching the AXRCockpit app on the tablet, select the CONNECT button.
6. Enter the IP and Port displayed on the HMD into the tablet and select Yes.
7. The connection is complete when the "Connected!" toast pop-up appears on the tablet.

Troubleshooting

"Paring failed" during Pairing

• Check if the HMD and tablet are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
• Check if the "Pair device with pairing code" pop-up on the HMD has not been closed.
• Go to the Recent menu on the tablet, close the app, and then restart it.

"Not connected!" when selecting a button

• Check the Paring and Connect status between the HMD and the tablet.

"Connected!" then "Disconnected!" when selecting the Connect button

• Go to the Recent menu on the tablet, close the app, and then restart it.
• Check if the same pop-up appears after connecting.
• If the same pop-up appears, reboot the HMD and try again.
